# Brand products: creators and links

- Scope: the owned product catalogue and its product-scoped Creators & links drawer and standalone page. Mode: Operate. This brief records the implemented surface within the existing Xurjin world; it does not replace global DESIGN.md or its sidecar.
- Audience and job: approved brands find products with or without creator links, inspect the creators and individual historical links behind each product, compare recorded performance, inspect accepted terms, and manage one specific link.
- Authority: the user-pinned `C:/Users/mefar/Downloads/Xurjin-Products-Creator-Links-Implementation-Guide.md` and `01-products-desktop-mobile.png` / `02-creators-links-desktop-mobile.png` under `C:/Users/mefar/Downloads/Xurjin-Product-Links-Handoff/Xurjin-Product-Links-Handoff/references`. These establish composition and density. Their sample identities, counts, and money figures are illustrative, not product facts.
- Direction: extend the incumbent navy workspace shell with a compact, ruled product table, small identifying photographs, explicit creator/link counts, and a contextual creator hierarchy. The catalogue remains a management surface with product editing separate from link inspection.
- Visual contract: inherit the shared ink, paper, slate, signal-blue and worded status tokens and existing bilingual sans typography. Pale workspace backgrounds, white surfaces, fine separators and restrained blue actions carry the hierarchy. The catalogue title uses 29px bold type on desktop and 24px on mobile; ordinary content uses 14px. Financial figures use tabular numerals. Standard controls have a 44px minimum height, gently rounded 7px corners, and containers use 12px corners. Selected coverage is identified by a visible blue outline and `aria-pressed`, not colour alone. Product and creator images come from records, with supported fallbacks for missing media.
- Catalogue composition: title and Add product precede product/SKU search and reporting period. Coverage choices sit above the desktop filter rail and results. Table columns are Product, Current offer, Creators & links, Confirmed sales, Sales value and Actions. Product identity includes a small thumbnail, linked title, worded lifecycle status and exact GEL price. Creator count and total link count remain separate, with active-link count underneath. Explicit View creators and product overflow actions avoid a conflicting whole-row click target. Zero-link products retain inspection and editing actions and show their zero counts with an explanatory label.
- Filter contract: search, coverage, product status, product type, commission type, sorting and pagination are URL-backed. Search is debounced by 300ms. Coverage is single-select: All products, With active links, No links yet, and Only disabled links. Counts respect search and other catalogue filters while excluding the selected coverage condition. Commission type appears when multiple types exist. Filters combine; changing search, filtering or sorting resets pagination. Removable chips and Clear all expose applied state. Sorting supports newest, confirmed sales, sales value, creator count and name.
- Responsive catalogue: above 1100px, the table and left filter rail remain visible. At 1100px and below they become two-column compact product cards and a Filters trigger; at 700px and below cards form one column. Mobile search spans the width, coverage moves into the filter sheet, and labelled two-column sales metrics remain visible on each card. The bottom sheet edits draft coverage, status, type, commission type and sort; Reset changes that draft and Show results commits it. Closing without applying retains committed filters. Bottom spacing accounts for persistent navigation and device safe areas.
- Navigation contract: catalogue-to-creators navigation opens the intercepted right drawer at viewport widths of at least 1280px. The drawer is 860px wide, constrained to leave at least 260px beside it, fills the viewport height and uses a subdued scrim. Open full page, direct navigation and refresh provide the standalone destination. Below 1280px, catalogue links load the standalone page; crossing below that width while the drawer is open reloads to that page. The return URL carries catalogue state, while session storage records its scroll position for restoration. The standalone creator content is capped at 1120px.
- Creator composition: product identity, status, Current offer and explicitly labelled Price precede Creators & links, unfiltered Product totals and reporting period. Three summary panels show Confirmed sales, Sales value and Confirmed creator commission. Creator or link-code search, raw link-status filter and creator sort precede independently expandable creator groups. Parent rows show identity, profile access where available, link counts and scoped activity, plus View performance. Children preserve every link record and display code/copy action, worded status, accepted terms, clicks, confirmed sales, commission and link-specific overflow.
- Responsive creators: at 700px and below, the standalone view uses stacked creator cards and expanded link cards with visible field labels. Search, status and sort occupy full-width rows. Summary panels use two columns with commission spanning the row. Creator cards retain link count, confirmed sales and commission; detailed clicks remain in the expanded links. Long names, link values and accepted terms wrap. Back to products and bottom safe-area spacing preserve the surrounding workspace navigation.
- Metric contract: creator and link counts are lifetime record totals, independent of reporting period; creators are distinct IDs, links are individual records, and active/disabled counts use raw stored `ACTIVE` / `DISABLED` state. A raw active count does not promise current eligibility. Activity uses the canonical selected-period reporting definitions, including cancellation handling, and is scoped to the current brand and product. Confirmed creator commission does not imply payment. Missing metrics display an explicit unavailable state and retry rather than a fabricated zero.
- Filtered creator scope: creator-name search retains that creator's links matching status, while link-code search narrows individual links within creator groups. Filtered summary and parent metrics aggregate all matching links, including those beyond the visible child page, and are labelled Filtered results. Product totals remain unfiltered; the result count describes matching creators and links. A period without activity does not remove creators with historical links. Performance destinations carry product, creator/link where applicable, and period scope.
- Historical truth: group creators once while preserving their individual links without deduplication. Read-only accepted terms come from each link's stored snapshot and saved rate, with version when present. Missing historical fields are explicitly unavailable; today's offer never fills them. Product status, partnership approval and raw link status remain separate facts.
- Link lifecycle: Disable link opens a confirmation naming the selected link and explaining the forward-looking consequence. The mutation is scoped to that link and product ownership. History remains intact. Restore is offered only when eligibility permits it; otherwise a concrete reason is shown. Mutation guards prevent duplicate submission, feedback announces success or failure, and the view refreshes after success.
- Interaction contract: drawer, draft filter sheet, accepted-terms view and disable confirmation use named native modal dialogs. Nested Escape handling closes the current dialog without dismissing its parent. Dialog cleanup restores the previous body-scroll setting and focus to the connected trigger or containing overflow summary. Creator disclosure exposes expanded state; result and mutation notices use live status or alert semantics. Clipboard failure offers a selectable URL. Disclosure motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Georgian and English receive the same hierarchy and wrapping space.
- States: distinguish an empty catalogue, no filtered products, a product with no historical links, no matching creators/links, unavailable analytics, missing stored terms, missing media, pending navigation and failed mutations. Empty link history explains that creators generate links explicitly; inspecting or filtering never creates links or changes partnerships.
