# Performance workspace

- Scope: creator performance at `app/[locale]/creator/earnings/page.tsx`, brand performance at `app/[locale]/brand/performance/page.tsx`, and contextual snapshots from both tracking-link surfaces. Mode: Operate.
- Audience and job: creators understand every measurable outcome attributable to their links across brands, products, and exact links; brands understand the corresponding commercial result across products and creators.
- Direction: the replacement Xurjin analytics workspace from the user-supplied dashboard references. A factual period statement, linked white metric cards, trend and product-distribution panels lead to detailed source breakdowns. The prior Performance Pulse comp is historical and no longer visual authority.
- Provenance contract: aggregate figures deep-link to the rows that produced them. The drill path is total to brand or creator, product, tracking link, click, order, and commission. Confirmed never implies paid.
- Metric contract: recorded clicks count redirect events; converted clicks count distinct cited clicks; attributed orders count distinct conversions with matching attributions; converted-click rate is converted clicks divided by recorded clicks; sales use attributed commissionable net; earnings use stored attribution commission grouped by conversion status.
- Filter contract: URL-backed date period, brand/creator, product, link, and conversion status filters persist across performance, link, and earnings-ledger destinations. Default period is the last 30 days in Asia/Tbilisi.
- Visual contract: white rounded cards on the pale workspace canvas, navy rail, blue-purple accents, colored metric icons and tabular Manrope/Noto Sans Georgian figures. Six analytics cards use three desktop columns and two mobile columns. Chart panels keep report labels and source destinations visible.
- Responsive contract: metric cards become a two-column readout, the chart retains labeled values, and breakdown tables become complete stacked source rows without horizontal page overflow. Georgian and English keep equal hierarchy and room.
- Distribution contract: the donut uses positive attributed sales by product from the report, up to four leading products and a combined Other segment; labels and percentages accompany the chart. No attributed sales produce an explanatory empty state.
- Evidence: recorded from current source; final browser validation is tracked separately in `.impeccable/review/xurjin`.
- States: no links, links without clicks, clicks without conversion, pending-only earnings, cancelled earnings, disabled links with historical results, and filtered no-match results all receive explicit copy.
