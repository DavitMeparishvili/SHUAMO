# Outcome workspaces

The supplied Xurjin redesign brief governs behavior; the five PDF boards govern composition. Preserve the navy shell, white working surfaces, cobalt actions and Manrope/Noto Sans Georgian typography. Lead creators with net earned commission and brands with confirmed attributed sales. Follow money with next actions, measured trends and rankings. Keep source records behind contextual drilldowns. Use role-scoped data only; illustrative mockup values must never enter production rendering.

Desktop uses compact outcome tiles and two-column trend/ranking sections. Mobile stacks content, keeps the date range visible, batches filters in a bottom sheet and expands sale detail to the full screen. No one-slice donuts, unsupported conversion rate or payment-balance terminology. See `docs/xurjin-redesign-handoff.md` for implementation and validation.
