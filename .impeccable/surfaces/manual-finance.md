# Manual finance and admin reporting

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing Xurjin workspace; preserve owner performance outside the finance composition slot.

Authority: user-approved September 13 manual-finance plan/spec, Downloads/Xurjin-Manual-Finance-UI-Guide (1).md, and the corresponding handoff reference boards and report-source assets. This is a supplied-design implementation; no new visual-world exploration is required.

Primary tasks: admin finds accounts, records external transfers, reviews immutable invoices, and inspects read-only product/performance reports. Owners understand what remains, what was earned and what was recorded. CSV exports all matching rows.

Design: existing navy shell and logo; Finance administrator / FA identity; Finance, Products, Performance destinations. Pale canvas, white 12px panels, thin cool borders, cobalt actions. Use existing Manrope and Georgian fonts. Mobile menu shell, stacked financial summaries and labeled report cards; forms use a side panel on desktop and full-screen dialog on mobile.

First viewport: page identity, focused action, tabs/filters, and the primary account balance or commercial totals. Avoid inherited grid gaps that push primary data below the fold. Financial scope explanations remain adjacent to their numbers.

Signature interaction: editable transfer amount previews the exact resulting balance, including credit, without imposing a payment policy. Generating an invoice changes no balance. Chart sales and commission on separate aligned zero-based axes with exact accessible data.

Finance-specific colors from the supplied report CSS: #10213d text, #526680 secondary text, #dce3ee borders, #2f5fe8 primary, #0b172b shell. Credit #176653; commission chart #7854cb. Maintain the reference composition while allowing genuine data and Georgian labels to wrap.

Verification artifacts: .impeccable/review/finance-*, overview-*, products-*, product-detail-*, payment-*, invoice-*, owner-* screenshots. Fresh-context reviewer reports findings directly to implementer.
