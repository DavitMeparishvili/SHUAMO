# Design System: Xurjin

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Connected commerce"**

Xurjin connects a confident public invitation with a clear commerce workspace. The user-selected reference world combines a navy globe, blue-to-purple light, geometric X identity and human photography with white working surfaces, compact metrics and a navy navigation rail.

This replaces the former SHUAMO visual identity. The six supplied screenshots govern the direction; the implemented source governs the exact tokens below. Reference gradients are intentional brand elements, including highlighted hero text, primary calls to action, rail depth and soft supporting panels.

Extracted from app/xurjin.css, app/discovery.css, app/globals.css, app/tokens.css, src/modules/design-system and the implemented shell, registration, overview, performance and discovery components. This document records source behavior; it does not certify a final browser or accessibility pass. The previously selected mockups are historical records and do not override the replacement screenshots.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Navy framing and white, softly rounded working cards.
- A blue-purple geometric X with the Latin Xurjin name in both locales.
- Self-hosted Manrope and Noto Sans Georgian with tabular financial figures.
- Human audience imagery, a globe backdrop, and measured motion.

## Colors

Deep navy anchors the composition; electric blue, cyan and purple provide the reference's recognizable light. The machine-readable palette includes source token names and descriptive names for directly authored surface colors, including chart series and opacity overlays.

### Primary

Signal blue carries links, focus and solid operational actions. Gradient blue and purple form the shared primary-action gradient (110deg). Hero cyan, blue and purple form highlighted hero text (100deg, with the blue stop at 50%). Logo cyan, blue and purple belong to the fixed geometric X.

Product discovery has a scoped solid discovery accent, darker hover and pale blue supporting fill. These tokens color its actions, selected filters and commission panels. The shared Xurjin identity, marketing gradients and other workspace accents retain their established roles.

Manual finance uses its scoped cobalt for solid actions, links, active tab underlines and the sales chart. Its credit green supplements explicit Credit or Paid above current earnings labels; the scoped commission purple identifies the separate commission chart. Finance navy frames the administrator rail. These four supplied-reference colors extend the existing workspace palette only within finance and admin reporting; finance ink, muted text and borders preserve the scoped handoff tones. Other supporting surfaces retain their local tonal equivalents and shared state tokens.

### Secondary

Soft cyan and lavender washes organize audience steps, quick actions and Coming soon panels. Chart colors identify series alongside legends; their presence does not imply financial state.

### Tertiary

Accrued amber, settled green and reversed red retain their worded financial-state roles. Registration success and error surfaces have their own extracted supporting tones.

### Neutral

Navy and navy-soft frame marketing and navigation. Ink is the dark heading color; slate-700 carries body text; slate-500 is muted copy. Paper carries forms and cards on slate-50 workspace backgrounds, with slate-200 separators and slate-300 control borders. Other extracted colors describe localized rail, field, chart and photographic-overlay treatments rather than a new universal palette.

**The Reference Gradient Rule.** Retain the user-selected blue-to-purple gradients in their established roles; do not replace them with the discarded single-indigo palette.

**The Words With State Rule.** Every status and unavailable feature needs a visible word label. Color and icons supplement its meaning.

## Typography

Profiles inherit the shared Manrope and Noto Sans Georgian stack. The profile-only creator name uses the 44px heading role on desktop and 38px on mobile; brand names use 34px and 28px. Editor headings step from 28px to 24px below 1100px and 18px below 760px. These reference-led sizes are local hierarchy choices, not replacements for shared headings.

Manrope is the Latin family; Noto Sans Georgian is the Georgian glyph fallback in the same stack. Both are bundled through Fontsource CSS imports. The Xurjin wordmark uses Manrope.
